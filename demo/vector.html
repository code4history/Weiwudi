How to verify the cache
  1. Move and zoom the map
  2. Watch the statistics grow (tiles, glyphs, sprites)
  3. Open DevTools → Network, check "Offline", then reload this page
  4. The map you moved over still draws (no blank canvas)
  5. Uncheck "Offline" to continue

Map data: OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles Data from OpenStreetMap (attribution is required; MapLibre shows it on the map). The style is Liberty from OpenFreeMap. Their terms of service ask offline downloads to send a User-Agent that identifies the app. Weiwudi adds its User-Agent only to same-origin upstreams, and OpenFreeMap is on another origin, so this demo does not download ranges in bulk: it only caches what you view while moving the map (up to 50 MB). Range downloads are shown in the raster demo. To load this page offline, reload it once online first (so that the service worker controls it).
