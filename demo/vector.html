How to verify the cache
  1. Move and zoom the map
  2. Watch the statistics grow (tiles, glyphs, sprites)
  3. Open DevTools → Network, check "Offline", then reload this page
  4. The map you moved over still draws (no blank canvas)
  5. Uncheck "Offline" to continue

Map data: OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles Data from OpenStreetMap (attribution is required; MapLibre shows it on the map). The style is Liberty from OpenFreeMap. Their terms of service allow offline downloads of at most 3,000 tiles or 50 MB, made with at most 4 parallel requests and sent with a User-Agent that identifies the app. A browser cannot change its User-Agent, so this demo keeps well below the limit: it refuses more than 50 tiles at once, fetches with 4 parallel requests, and stops caching at 50 MB. To load this page offline, reload it once online first (so that the service worker controls it).
